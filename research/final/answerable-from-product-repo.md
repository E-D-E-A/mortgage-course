**The five findings that matter most.**
1. **No mix, payment, ratio, LTV or 18-month calculation exists anywhere in the repo.** The only calculation is the monthly-income average (net pay, any number of months) and three reader checks. The API has no client for the calculations app (`grep` finds the reader client only). So "אין חישוב תמהיל בקוד" is confirmed. But `CLAUDE.md` line 13 lists **ENG-223 as the next issue** (not frozen); the freeze (2026-10-08, Backlog) comes from Linear via `research/source-map.md:52`.

The reader is not "in assisted mode" anywhere in code; there is no documents screen; the Claude arm is "not built".

The upload route and the reading job have **no consent check** (grep: none), the consent step is off the map (Eden, 2026-10-03). The hard line in the repo is narrower than the course's version (reader only; "the client", not "all signers"; adds lawyer review of the payslip definition).
4. **Net vs net-to-pay (ש24):** Mogi's payslip definition reads "שכר נטו", explicitly not "נטו לתשלום", and records that BIZ-38 must still confirm which net each bank uses. One-off payments are only *flagged*, not excluded.
