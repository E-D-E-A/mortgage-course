# Fix pass 2026-10-18: new open questions from stages 4 and 5

Added by the stage 4-5 fix pass. Nothing here is in the lessons as a to-do; each is already shown to the learner as "שאלה פתוחה" or "לא אומת".

1. The 40% payment-to-income line: is it written in Directive 329 or in Directive 203? Secondary sources (a financial article and a consumer guide) say 329 bars above 50% and above 40% the loan carries a 100% risk weight (a capital cost). Needs the primary text of 329 and 203 section 72. Lessons 4.4, 4.5, 5.10.
2. Age limit at the end of the term: practice found in a secondary guide is repayment by age 80 of the oldest borrower or 30 years, 83-85 in some banks; no Bank of Israel rule found. Which banks, and is it the oldest borrower? Lesson 4.11 (card "term").
3. Pre-approval validity: 24 days is supported by a search summary of circular 2540 and a Hapoalim guide; are the days calendar days, and does a new document reset the count? Lessons 4.7, 4.11, 5.1, 5.10.
4. Exactly 18 months left on a commitment: counted or not (Directive 329 appendix A)? Lessons 4.2, 4.6, 4.15.
5. Rent that ends when the borrowers move into the purchased home: the course assumes it is not counted (a course assumption, no source). Lesson 4.2.
6. Three uniform baskets and the 31.8.2022 start: confirmed by three secondary sources, still not read in Directive 451 appendix 5. Lesson 5.2.
7. Circular 2852: a search summary says it moved the start of an amendment to Directive 329 (the definition of a housing loan) to 1.10.2026. Content not opened. Lessons 4.3, 4.4, 4.14.
