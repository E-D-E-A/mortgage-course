Ten most valuable findings:
1. **Rate-decision dates (C-O3) verified**: BoI official calendar page (via search snippet): 21 Oct 2026 (with forecast + press conference) and 23 Nov 2026 (rate effective 26 Nov). Course's "21 Oct, 23 Nov" is right; the course's "לא נבדקו" can become verified (still via snippet, page not opened).
2. **The 40% line is a capital line, not a ban** (A-A2, A-B1, D-2): secondary sources (a Globes report, a summary of Directive 329 texts) say PTI above 40% means 100% risk weight (Directive 203 mechanism, referenced in 329) and PTI above 50% is forbidden. The course's wording "סף הון" is consistent. BUT one press piece (Bizportal) says banks "stop around a third to 40%", which supports FK-H-012 only as press folklore, not as policy.
3. **A possible error in the 451 baseline**: the course (C-C4, D-5) says 24 days for pre-approval; secondary summaries of the Nov 2017 circular confirm 24 days (was 12) **but 12 days remain for loans that need no new lien** (section 4ג(ה)). The course must not say "24 for everyone".
4. **Directive 253 (April 2026)** changes 451 deadlines (e.g. "3 business days" to "8", a "consider extending pre-approvals" clause). Course text that cites 451 deadlines (5/7 business days, 24/12 days) must say which version; the notes already flag 253 as unread. Nobody can resolve this without 253_2.pdf.
5. **No source supports "inquiries from many banks hurt the credit score"** (C-C2, D-7). Search snippets only say banks use a credit indication (חיווי) with customer's knowledge. Verdict: not verified in either direction; the course's "שאלה פתוחה" is correct; do not teach "4 banks hurts".
6. **FK-H-004 band conflict is real but irrelevant**: BoI series bands (30/45/60/75/90) are statistics; the 40/60/75 are capital/policy lines. A-A5 stays B. Course must keep it as hypothesis.
7. **Mortgage-advisor conflict of interest**: the Capital Market Authority draft (reported in press) would bar non-bank credit companies from paying fees/benefits to mortgage advisers; Directive 329A is about the *bank's* policy on working with client representatives, not advisor fees. Relevant to any line in the course on who pays Mogi. Classification C.
8. **Gift vs savings** (B-3, B-16, F-3): nothing official found; press (klikatnadlan) says banks want equity to be equity not debt and typically use a signed gift declaration. That supports a *question for Eylon* and a *lawyer warning*, not a course fact.
9. **Elections Tuesday 27 Oct 2026** is reported in several outlets (Maariv, Globes); the final date in law was not confirmed from an official site. Banks closed on election day: standard (it is a statutory rest day) but not verified here. C-O4: partly A.
10. **Prime moves with BoI by definition (prime = BoI rate + 1.5), margin fixed** is confirmed by secondary sources (nadlancenter, hamichlol). What a bank *holds* after pre-approval (C-C10, D-5, F-2) is not in any snippet: D, open 451 section 4ג.
