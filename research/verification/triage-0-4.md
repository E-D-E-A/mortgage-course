**Top findings (places the course is wrong or needs a fix):**
1. **Pre-approval validity is 24 days, not "3 months" (FK-H-027).** BoI circular 2540 (Nov 2017) changed 12 to 24 days in Directive 451 §4ג(א). Whether the days are calendar days: not verified.
2. **Mizrahi's share is about 37%, not 54% (FK-H-098).** Mizrahi's own investor presentation (31.12.2025): 36.7%; Funder: 37.1% of 2025 originations; Leumi 23.1%, Poalim 21.4%, Discount 11.3%.
3. **"Law of 2016" for age 70/80 does not exist as far as found (FK-H-006).** Practice: repayment by age 80 of the oldest borrower or 30 years; 70 appears only in life insurance.
4. **The 5-7 day answer is a Bank of Israel directive from the 31.8.2022 reform, not a "2021 law" (FK-H-024).**
5. **The prime-share limit (one third) was cancelled in December 2020 (FK-H-060).** What remains: at least one third fixed.
6. **LTV vs price vs appraisal (FK-H-005): the ratio is measured against the lower of price and appraisal.** Higher appraisal means the price counts (BoI Q&A snippet). Exception: reduced-price dwellings (329 §4א(2)).
7. **The 40% line is in Directive 329 (it removes the §72 relief) and the 100% risk weight is in Directive 203 (answers an open question).** The 50% cap is a ban; 40% is a capital cost, not a ban. Kolzchut says the ratio includes mortgage insurance and related costs.
8. **27.10.2026 (election day) is a closed bank day** in BoI's 2026 business-day list. Course can drop "לא אומת".
9. **Circular 2852 identified:** it extends the start date of an amendment to Directive 329 (the definition of "housing loan" and Q&A 5.1) to 1.10.2026, so it is already in force; its content is unknown. Open it.
10. **"40/60/75" LTV tiers (FK-H-004) were not found anywhere;** do not present as BoI figures. Also "the only country" in the 2008 crisis (FK-H-103) is wrong: OECD, Canada, Australia.

**Not found / needs opening (D):** Directive 329 Appendix A (18 months, the definition of liabilities), Directive 203 §72, Directive 451 §§4ג, 9(ה), 11, 15ב1, appendix 4, the Credit Data Law and regulations, the BoI 2005 business-day definition and its application in 451.
