Top findings:
1. **FK-H-031 (Eylon: "פריים, קבועה צמודה, קבועה לא צמודה", "סוף 2020") is wrong on both counts.** Three independent secondary sources (BoI explainer via search, Mizrahi/Bizportal, BoI Nov-2021 press release via Globes) give the baskets as: 100% fixed unindexed; 1/3 fixed unindexed + 1/3 variable-indexed (every 5 years, government-bond anchor) + 1/3 prime; 50% fixed unindexed + 50% prime. This matches the team transcript of Appendix 5. Date: BoI press release URL says the reform "will go into effect tomorrow (31/8/22)"; announced Nov 2021. The course's 2022-08-31 is consistent (still read the primary).
2. The BoI explainer states the purpose of the uniform baskets as **comparison** (against the bank's own offers and other banks). No source found supports "הצעת דמה"; no source found refutes pricing behavior either. Course is right not to teach "decoy"; pricing stays "לא ידוע" (B).
3. **"3 חודשים" validity folklore**: Hapoalim's mortgage guide (via search) says the rate in the approval is valid **24 days from the approval date**, and after an extension the bank may change the rate; Midrag Q&A says approval 30–45 days / credit framework 3 months (secondary, low). Supports the course's "מיום שהאישור ניתן"; the "12 days" rule was not found.
4. **"פריים = ריבית בנק ישראל + 1.5"** is a universal convention in secondary sources (hon.co.il, Midrag, ICE); no source ties it to Directive 451. Course's "convention, not a rule of 451" is right; can be upgraded from "unverified" to "convention confirmed by secondary sources" (B10/C12/12 resolve).
5. **The 40% figure** (C4/D5/FK-H-012 vs J-090): a 2026 Bizportal/Hon snippet says Directive 329 (June 2026 version) bars approving a housing loan above **50%** repayment ratio, and above **40%** the loan gets a **100% risk weight** (capital). This reconciles "banks stop at 40 in practice" with "40 is a price/capital line, not a refusal" — a capital-weight line. Medium confidence; open 329/203 to confirm which one holds it.
6. **Directive 329 snippet (Oct 2026-dated Hon item)**: the repayment-ratio calculation was updated to count an existing mortgage's repayment when applying for any loan secured on a dwelling (not first-time buyers with no prior housing debt). Course text on the ratio should be checked against the current 329 (LLM-search paraphrase, medium-low).
7. **Fee "360 ₪"**: the only source found for 360 ₪ is a **private-member bill** (Haim Katz/Eti Atiya) to cap the mortgage-application fee at 360 ₪, status reported inconsistently, vs. banks' reported 0.25% of the deal. The course uses 360 ₪ as an assumption in examples — make sure it is not read as law. (A7/A14.)
8. **FK-H-060** (prime cap repealed Dec 2020, circular 2647): a BoI file `h2647_en.pdf` exists and surfaced for this query, but its content was not seen; a 2011 BoI document shows the old one-third variable cap. Keep "לא אומת".
9. Directive 451 EN PDF notes **only the Hebrew version is binding**; a Sept-2024 Hebrew draft circular proposed further amendments to 451 (finalization unknown) — the transcript (v22, 19.7.2023) may be outdated. Verify the current version before stating any 451 detail as "current".
10. BIR_MRTG_99 definition (effective vs contractual; weighted vs simple) could not be found by search; remains D. A 2016 Globes snippet says BoI's headline figure blends fixed and floating — do not describe it as a single-product rate.
