**Top 10 findings (places where the course is wrong, incomplete, or confirmed):**
1. **Directive 329 was amended recently**: circulars of 16.12.2025 (clarifies how the payment-to-income ratio is computed), 10.5.2026 (definition of "הלוואה לדיור"), a Q&A file of 8.2.2026 (202601.pdf), and a version dated 06/26. The course's "תעתיק הצוות" of 329 may predate these. Re-open before locking 4.x, 6.9, 8.6–8.8, 9.x numbers (rows 8E-E6/E7, 9C-C3..C5, 9A-A11).
2. **Course "40%" is consistent with public sources**: statutory payment-to-income ceiling is 50%, but above 40% the bank must hold a 100% risk weight (bizportal, citing 329 of 06/2026), so 40% is a *pricing/capital* line, not a refusal line. Supports the course's own wording; explains FK-H-012 (8D-D5) with a public mechanism. Medium confidence.
3. **Directive 329 confirmed from search summaries**: LTV 75% (single), 70% (replacement), 50% (investment); variable part up to 2/3 (66.7%); max 30 years; PTI 50%. These can move from "לא אומת" to "ממקור משני" (7D-5, 8A-A4 partly, 8E-E7, 9C-C4).
4. **LTV counts every lien on the property regardless of lender**: BoI Q&A (202601) says the calculation includes existing loans secured on the purchased property; a 2014 circular says the same for prior loans on the same dwelling (7C-C14 likely TRUE; course currently "לפי תקציר, לא אומת").
5. **Temporary directive exists and the course sentence is right** (7C-C9): BoI Supervisor press release 23.3.2025, temporary order until end-2026, extra capital where >25% of a project's contracts defer payment, contractor-loan cap 10% of monthly housing-loan volume; circular C-06-2816 (2025) appears in a regalert listing. The course may add these details; it may not say it limits *clients*.
6. **Internal refinance fee (7B-9, 7D-3, 7A)**: FIBI's early-repayment sheet is reported to state an exemption when the bank itself grants a new loan to repay the old one; scope (which fee) unclear. The course says flatly "לא ידוע"; that may be too weak. Open FIBI משכ-209 and the order §3. No source found saying the discounting fee is waived.
7. **Notice (הודעה מוקדמת)**: Hapoalim sheet: notice valid 6 months; if you gave notice and did not repay you lose the waiver for repayment within 6 months of the notified date. Not in the course; relevant to 7.3 and 7B-8.
8. **Exit station**: Mercantile sheet states that on the rate-change day only the operational fee is charged (matches course 7A-A5). Discount/Leumi/Mercantile show 20% off after 3 yrs, 30% after 5 yrs; Mercantile also shows a 10% tier in year 2: the course's reduction table must say *which* loans (resolve against the order §8).
9. **Index fee (7A-A9)**: one bank sheet describes it as repayment between the 1st and 15th × half of the average change of the last 12 published indices (probably a non-housing sheet; unconfirmed for the housing order). Helps the open "11 or 12 changes / arithmetic" question only partially.
10. **Advisor conflict of interest (9B-B4, 9C-C7)**: public: a Capital Market Authority draft circular would bar non-bank lenders from paying commissions/benefits to mortgage advisors; the advisor-licensing bill passed first-reading committee approval on 26.5.2026 (klikatnadlan, secondary). Whether *banks* pay advisors: one article claims banks may not reward referrals; unverified. Keep class C.
